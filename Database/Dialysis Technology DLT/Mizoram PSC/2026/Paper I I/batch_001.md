Option B: Blood flow >600ml/min
Option C: Diameter < 6 mm
Option D: Access vessel depth < 6mm
Correct Answer: Option C
Difficulty: Medium
Explanation: The KDOQI "Rule of 6s" for mature, cannulatable AV fistulas specifies: access blood flow >600\text{ mL/min}, vein diameter \ge 6\text{ mm} (not <6\text{ mm}), depth <6\text{ mm} beneath skin, and evaluated 6 weeks post-surgery.
Source: DIALYSIS TECHNOLOGIST UNDER HEALTH & FAMILY WELFARE DEPARTMENT, MIZORAM PSC JANUARY-2026 Paper 2
