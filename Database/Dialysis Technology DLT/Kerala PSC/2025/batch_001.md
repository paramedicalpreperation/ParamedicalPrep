## Q1
Question: Which of the following is a purely convection-based treatment modality?
Option A: Hemodialysis
Option B: Hemofiltration
Option C: Plasmapheresis
Option D: Hemadsorption
Correct Answer: Option B
Difficulty: Easy
Explanation: Hemofiltration is a renal replacement therapy that relies entirely on convection (solvent drag) across a high-flux membrane driven by a transmembrane pressure gradient, mimicking glomerular ultrafiltration without using dialysate fluid.

## Q2

Option A: KA
Option B: KUF
Option C: Kt/V
Option D: Urea reduction ratio
Correct Answer: Option A
Difficulty: Medium
Explanation: The efficiency of a dialyzer is defined by its mass transfer area coefficient (K_A or K_oA), which reflects the maximum theoretical clearance capacity of the dialyzer membrane for a specific solute (like urea) at infinite blood and dialysate flow rates.
