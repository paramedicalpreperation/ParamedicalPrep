Question: Cysticercosis in man is due to depostition of larvae of:
Option A: Taenia solium
Option B: Zona saginata
Option C: Taenia multiceps
Option D: Taenia asiatica
Correct Answer: Option A
Difficulty: Easy
Explanation: Human cysticercosis is an infection caused by the larval tissue stage (*Cysticercus cellulosae*) of the pork tapeworm, *Taenia solium*, acquired via accidental ingestion of infective *T. solium* eggs.
